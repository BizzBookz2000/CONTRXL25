Welcome to CONTRXL 25

Keyboard sampling workstation · version __VERSION__

This installer puts CONTRXL 25 in your Applications folder. It runs on Intel and Apple silicon Macs with macOS 10.13 or newer.

  • 64 pads, a 25-key (or larger) keyboard, a 16-pattern sequencer and song chain
  • Sampling with threshold and level meter, chopping, pads on keys
  • Audio Unit and VST3 instruments and effects, with safe plug-in scanning

Installing a new version over an older one keeps your songs, sample library and settings.
