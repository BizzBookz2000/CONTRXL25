CONTRXL 25 is installed

Open it from Applications, Launchpad or Spotlight.

  1. Click Audio / MIDI (top bar) and pick your output, your input and your MIDI keyboard.
  2. The first time you press Sample, macOS asks for microphone access. Click Allow.
  3. To use your plug-ins: Mixer tab → Plug-ins... → Scan all...

The User's Manual is in the disk image this installer came from.
